## Trang chủ desktop toàn chiều dài

Tệp: [05-v2-home-artistic-full.png](mockups/05-v2-home-artistic-full.png).

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: a complete LONG scrolling desktop homepage mockup for VNStore, a Vietnamese product discovery website. A SINGLE whole page, front-facing flat screenshot, portrait canvas roughly 1200x3000 or largest supported tall portrait, no devices, no perspective.
Primary request: exceptionally artistic Vietnamese editorial art direction. A cultural art journal merged with a usable contemporary catalog. Deliberately composed asymmetry, exquisite negative space, cutout photographic objects, expressive Vietnamese serif headlines, delicate ink-drawn botanical details, alternating ivory paper and deep forest green sections. The visual identity must be DISTRIBUTED ALL THE WAY FROM TOP TO FOOTER, not a beautiful hero followed by generic UI. Do not put all Vietnamese objects in the hero.
Palette: chalky warm ivory #F5F0E5, deep forest green #183E33, ink black green, subtle straw gold, tiny cinnabar #A84432 editorial accents, very restrained dusty lotus pink. Fine paper impression, not dirty or distressed. Editorial art catalogue quality, contemporary Vietnamese gallery sensibility. Hairline rules, square-edged photographs, no repetitive rounded card boxes. Legible modern sans-serif controls supporting Vietnamese.
MANDATORY whole-page layout with SIX distinct chapters visible in this single image, each about one sixth of the page:
1 TOP 0–17%: small elegant VNStore wordmark header and nav "Khám phá", "Làng nghề", "Thương hiệu", "Yêu thích". Spacious ivory typographic opening, very large artistic heading "Nếp Việt" with smaller line "Trong từng món đồ". Extremely subtle diagonal shadows of bamboo leaves across an EMPTY right-hand margin, one slender cropped ink bamboo branch entering from upper right, a small isolated ceramic vase offset near lower right. Wide usable search field underneath heading "Bạn đang tìm món gì?" with mic, camera, green "Tìm". This opening has NO conical hat, NO lotus pond, NO scene containing all motifs. Whitespace matters.
2 17–35%: "Những điều mộc mạc" — an asymmetric editorial category composition. A single large photographic authentic Vietnamese conical palm-leaf nón lá cutout is partially cropped at the LEFT page edge, separated from text and cards. At right an orderly but asymmetrically sized set of three photographic still-life windows: ceramic tea set, handwoven basket/bag, elegant ivory silk. Labels "Gốm sứ", "Mây tre đan", "Dệt may"; thin text links "Gỗ & sơn mài", "Đặc sản". Generous paper, small chapter number 01, a single cinnabar square editorial mark with no seal certification text. Hat belongs HERE, not hero.
3 35–52%: a deep forest green poetic pause with a wide richly photographic Vietnamese lotus pond on the RIGHT two thirds, delicately faded natural reflection at the lower edge, pink lotus blossoms and broad leaves. On LEFT solid dark green readable negative space, ivory serif title "Một khoảng an yên". A fine line of text and link "Khám phá". A tiny ink lotus stem elegantly crosses only the seam in unused margin. This is the only large lotus POND photograph, positioned MID-PAGE.
4 52–71%: return to ivory, heading "Đồ Việt, gần mình". Four clean product photographs with generous margins, no boxed UI card backgrounds: white ceramic tea set, authentic handwoven water-hyacinth bag, ivory silk scarf, packaged artisan chocolate. Neat short captions, "Giá tham khảo", small outlined favorite hearts. A narrow cropped macro photograph of real woven bamboo runs vertically only in the FAR RIGHT outer gutter, never behind product text. One small botanical ink flourish on the opposite margin. Content practical and legible.
5 71–88%: "Theo dấu bàn tay" with staggered editorial photography — close-up hands weaving natural bamboo on the LEFT, a warm quiet craft workbench smaller at upper RIGHT, thin caption rules and a few lines of type, action "Khám phá làng nghề". A narrow soft fabric edge enters from the far right blank margin to connect material story, not a random sticker. Do not invent maker names or geographic provenance. Images are conceptual craft illustrations.
6 88–100%: a deep green FOOTER art landscape: panoramic atmospheric ink-and-wash Vietnamese lotus leaves and distant bamboo silhouettes along the BOTTOM edge, empty solid dark upper area with VNStore wordmark, small nav and concise text. A small pale conical hat outline as a colophon at far right, not a second product shot. Footer is a designed final chapter, not a blank bar.
Constraints: all six zones must be visible and carefully proportioned; motif density is distributed, largest visual scene is in the middle. Real graphic design with aligned UI, selective overlaps only in decorative margins, quiet breathing spaces. No cart, checkout, buy button, stars, fake verified badges, fake prices, names of villages, temples, pagodas, red lanterns, pseudo-Asian calligraphy. Main Vietnamese text accurate, small body may be short and sparse. Avoid the standard split hero + rows of rounded cards template. No huge monolithic hero photo. No ornamental border surrounding every card.
```

## Hai trang con toàn chiều dài

Tệp: [06-v2-search-product-artistic.png](mockups/06-v2-search-product-artistic.png).

```text
Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: two complete long desktop inner-page mockups side by side, VNStore Vietnamese product discovery. Flat front-facing layouts, not devices. Portrait overall canvas about 1800x2300 so BOTH pages show header, middle, bottom and artistic footer. Small editorial labels above each "TÌM KIẾM" and "CHI TIẾT".
Primary request: artful Vietnamese cultural journal aesthetics consistently distributed across INNER PAGES, not a home-only decorative hero. Elegant asymmetrical compositions surrounding rigorously legible functional UI. Warm chalk ivory #F5F0E5, deep ink forest #183E33, a small cinnabar #A84432 square as editorial punctuation, straw and faded silk. Exquisite large Vietnamese serif headings, modern small sans-serif UI, fine rules, rectangular photo windows, generous paper space. Botanical ink etching, selective photographic material cutouts, refined collaged photography. No generic rounded box grids everywhere.
LEFT PAGE, SEARCH:
Compact wordmark VNStore top and simple nav. Short airy masthead with "Tìm một món hợp ý", delicate bamboo shadow ONLY at far upper left margin; search field "túi đan có quai" with mic, camera and green "Tìm". Then 2-column functional product results beside a narrow filter sidebar. Sidebar heading "Bộ lọc", simple labels "Chất liệu", "Khoảng giá". Product grid six distinct woven bags on clean backgrounds, small heart controls, short captions and "Giá tham khảo"; no numeric prices or origin claims. The content occupies upper through middle 65% of page.
Distributed art: at about 30% page height an authentic Vietnamese conical palm-leaf hat photographed in extreme cropped curved profile ENTERS the LEFT OUTER margin BELOW the filter heading, never covering controls. At about 60% height thin lotus botanical ink linework appears on RIGHT OUTER margin in blank gutter, different motif and scale from hat. Below results a quiet white band and "Xem thêm". At 75% height a narrow art editorial band pairs a macro bamboo-weave photograph on left with "Từ những sợi mộc" and small "Khám phá làng nghề" link on right. At bottom a deep forest footer with delicately drawn lotus leaves and bamboo silhouettes along lower edge, blank space for VNStore wordmark and navigation; NO big hero.
RIGHT PAGE, PRODUCT:
Compact same header, "Khám phá / Mây tre đan" breadcrumb. Large elegant photographed woven bag on ivory pedestal occupies upper LEFT; title "Túi đan có quai", "Giá tham khảo", deep green "Hỏi trợ lý" and heart "Yêu thích" on upper RIGHT. A few small gallery thumbnails below main image. Slight soft bamboo-leaf shadow falls on a blank outer margin, NEVER on the bag itself. No invented manufacturer.
Middle at 40% height: a broad quiet editorial heading "Chất liệu & cách làm" on left, short spaced ruled attribute rows on right with neutral labels, a small photograph of woven fibers set beneath the heading. A folded ivory silk ribbon photographic fragment occupies the far RIGHT page edge, separated from rows.
At 60% height: heading "Nguồn gốc và căn cứ", a tasteful warm paper evidence panel with readable label/value rows, "Chưa rõ" and "Xem nguồn", no ornate certificate or verified stamp. Beside this panel a thin low contrast lotus stem ink drawing entering from left outside reading area.
At 75% height: "Có thể bạn cũng thích", three small neutral product photographs of basket, ceramic pot and silk. Underneath "Chưa có đánh giá". At 90–100% bottom: designed forest-green footer with a fine wide Vietnamese lotus-water landscape, an authentic nón lá small line illustration rests near a bamboo shoreline, not floating like a boat.
Constraints: all cultural motifs must be paced along full page height with meaningful empty gaps; two different full-page compositions, consistent brand. Functional text and products never obscured. Artsy but usable e-commerce-discovery interface. NO cart, buy/checkout controls, sale tags, stars, certification seals, factual place names, temples, pagodas, neon, repeating ornate frames. No fake numeric prices. Main Vietnamese labels accurate; body sparse. Footer only real nav Khám phá / Làng nghề / Thương hiệu / Yêu thích, no social icons or year.
```

```text
Use case: ui-mockup

The entire scrolling page must feel designed with Vietnamese images distributed in distinct places. Keep controls readable and whitespace intentional. Warm ivory paper, deep forest green, tiny cinnabar marks, elegant high-contrast Vietnamese serif headlines and clean sans-serif UI, fine rules. Constraints: all 6 sections visible with composition adapted to narrow mobile. No giant hero swallowing the layout. Vietnamese text correct and sparse. Authentic nón lá, no pagodas/temples/lanterns. No checkout, sale labels, ratings, certification badges, numeric product prices. Exquisite cultural art book on a mobile website, not generic ecommerce templates or a scrapbook of many stickers.

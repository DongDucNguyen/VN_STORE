Our woven seagrass baskets are handmade in Vietnam from 100% natural seagrass, a renewable and eco-friendly material grown in the Mekong Delta. Each piece is carefully woven by skilled artisans, creating a durable and beautiful product that celebrates traditional Vietnamese craftsmanship.

These versatile baskets can be used as small storage baskets, flower pot holders, or decorative organizers for living rooms, bathrooms, and workspaces. Their natural color tones bring warmth and texture to any home décor style — from bohemian to minimalist.

At Vietnam Handicraft Co., Ltd, we offer custom sizes, shapes, and colors for wholesale and export orders. Perfect for retailers, importers, and home décor brands seeking sustainable handmade products.

Supplier & Manufacturer in Vietnam:
We provide high-quality, eco-friendly woven seagrass baskets directly from our workshops in Vietnam. Bulk and wholesale options are available for importers, home décor retailers, and distributors.
